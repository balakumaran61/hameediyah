The Kandar Counter

Walk the counter. Point. Pick. Pour.

Nasi kandar is ordered at the counter: rice first, then your pieces, then the kuah (curry gravy).

Slide along the steel trays, open a dish to read its story, and build a plate. When you're ready, show it to the staff. The counter is where it happens.

      Build your plate: one rice, up to 6 pieces, any kuah. The illustrated plate assembles as you add (HAM-13 art). The brass pole above it tilts as the plate gets heavier, and at 5+ pieces it tips with "Heavy plate! The pole is tipping." Reduced motion: the tilt snaps, no swing. Mobile: a bottom sheet with a count badge; tap the bar to open.
      Show at counter: full screen, black on white reversed for contrast, very large type, in English and Malay ("Saya nak:"), so staff can read it at arm's length across a busy counter. No checkout, no payment. ✕ or Esc returns to the menu.
