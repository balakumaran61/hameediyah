Why one long story plus one menu. The brand is a story about continuity, so it reads as one unbroken journey you carry forward, like the pole. Splitting Legacy and Visit into pages would break that thread. The menu is the one thing people come back for, so it gets its own route and is one tap away everywhere (SPEC §13 Q5).
Persistent UI. The progress pole doubles as the "where am I" indicator for a long page. On mobile the sticky bar keeps the two conversion actions (Menu, Directions) under the thumb at every point in the story.
