**Generation-count decision (HAM-03, 5 Oct 2026):**
- **Say:** "Seven generations" for the family and the business. Four sources support it, including the Malaysia Book of Records citation.
- **Say:** "Ahamed Seeni Pakir Abdul Shukor, who runs Hameediyah today".
- **Don't say** which generation the owner is. If a line needs it, use "the family's sixth and seventh generations run the counter today", which is true under either reading (S1 places both generations in the shop in 2019).
- **Why:** the sources disagree only on how the owner is counted, not on the total. Leaving his number out removes the risk of contradicting the family in front of them. Confirm with the family before hi-fi.

## Corrections to the brief (for SPEC §14.5 and HAM-21)

1. The founder came from **Ramanathapuram district, Tamil Nadu** (S1). No source says he sailed from Chennai. The voyage starts on "the Tamil Nadu coast"; IMG-11 and IMG-12 (Madras harbour) are captioned as period context, not as his port.
2. **Weld Quay** as the landing point appears only in the brief. Sources say "the docks at the nearby jetty".
3. **Crab Curry** is named in the brief but not found in any source as a Hameediyah dish. Keep it (it's a brief requirement) with `[VERIFY]`.
