- Food photos are stand-ins (SPEC §14.1). Any dish photo caption says "Representative photo".

## Glossary (first-use glosses)

| Word | Language | Gloss to use |
|------|----------|--------------|
| kandar | Malay | the shoulder pole that carried the food |
| nasi kandar | Malay | "pole rice": rice with curries, named for the pole |
| kuah | Malay | curry gravy |
| kuah campur | Malay | mixed gravies, ladled together over rice |
| banjir | Malay | "flood": rice drenched in mixed kuah |
| kuali | Malay | a wide wok |
| dulang | Malay | a large tray |
| kari ketam | Malay | crab curry |
| kurma | Malay (from Tamil/Urdu) | a mild, creamy curry |
| masala | Tamil / Hindi | the house spice blend |
| rempah | Malay | spice paste |
| ayam · kambing · daging · ikan · sotong | Malay | chicken · mutton · beef · fish · squid |
| bawang | Malay | onion |
| teh tarik | Malay | "pulled tea": frothy milk tea poured from a height |
| Lebuh | Malay | street (Lebuh Campbell = Campbell Street) |
